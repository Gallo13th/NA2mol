SOURCE DATA — sheet descriptions

Fig1                      Fig. 1 is a schematic of the NA2smol workflow; it contains no numerical data.
Fig2_method_metrics       Panels a, b, c, e: per-method metrics on the RNA test set and on DUD-E. One row per
                          method and benchmark: docking score (kcal mol-1), pose-relaxation RMSD (A), QED, SA
                          score, SEI, strain (kcal mol-1), screening retention (molecules and targets) and
                          target-level ECFP4-Tanimoto reference recovery (dimensionless, 0-1).
Fig2_scaffold_coverage    Panels g, h: non-chiral Bemis-Murcko scaffold counts after the +SEI gate, the matched-
                          library-size subsampling (counts and 2.5-97.5 percentile interval over 1,000 draws),
                          and per-target coverage of the screening cascade for each target and method.
Fig3_configurations       Panels a-f: the same metrics as Fig. 2 for the four training configurations (Baseline,
                          Pretrain, Pretrain+Constrain, full model) on the 27 shared RNA targets, together with
                          the per-target medians behind the docking and strain distributions and the waterfall
                          counts between successive tiers.
Fig3_recovery_curves      Panels b, g: fraction of eligible targets recovered as a function of the ECFP4-Tanimoto
                          threshold (0.20-0.50, step 0.05), for each method and each of the three reference-set
                          definitions (all actives, 27 targets; fine-tuning-similar actives removed, 18;
                          pretraining-library-similar actives removed, 14).
Fig4_scaffold_counts      Panel b: generated molecules for the 8HZE_A109 pocket with the size of the largest
                          sp2-connected system (atoms), the largest system threshold cut at 20 atoms, and the
                          Bemis-Murcko scaffold frequency ranking of the retained molecules.
Fig4_assay_values         Panels d, f: fluorescence competition dose-response values (compound concentration in uM,
                          normalised fluorescence, replicate values, fitted four-parameter logistic) and the
                          per-cell cellular fluorescence quantification expressed as a percentage of the
                          untreated control.
Fig5_ThT_values           Panel g: normalised thioflavin T fluorescence versus compound concentration, with the
                          fitted four-parameter logistic curve and the derived apparent DC50.
Fig5_qPCR_values          Panel h: KRAS transcript level relative to GAPDH, normalised to the untreated control,
                          for each concentration and replicate.
Table1_metric_values      Table 1: metric values per method for the DUD-E protein benchmark, the RNA test set and
                          the four training configurations, with the strain coverage percentages.
