\section*{Supplementary Table 6 | DNA sequences used in this study}
Oligonucleotides were purchased HPLC-purified from Sangon Biotech (Shanghai, China). The KRAS promoter G-quadruplex was annealed in 10~mM Tris--HCl (100~mM KCl, pH~7.4) at 90~$^\circ$C for 5~min and slowly cooled to 4~$^\circ$C; the primers were used for quantitative reverse-transcription PCR with GAPDH as the endogenous reference.
