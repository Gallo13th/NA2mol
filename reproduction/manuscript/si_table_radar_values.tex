\section*{Supplementary Table 12 | Normalised scores behind the panels of Figs.~2 and 3}
Values underlying Figs.~2a, 2d and 3a. In the radar of Fig.~3a each axis is normalised scores $s_m$ drawn at $r_m = 1 + 2s_m$, so the raw metric values are not recoverable from the figure and are given here. Property values are pooled medians over all of the method's molecules, the same molecule pool used by the retention-curve integrals; the radar axes for docking, RMSD, QED, SA, SEI and strain are the normalised integral of the retention curve (log$_{10}$ scale for strain), so a median is a summary and not the plotted quantity. Dock. is in kcal mol$^{-1}$, RMSD in \AA, and strain in kcal mol$^{-1}$; strain values above 10$^3$ are written in scientific notation. ECFP4 rec. is the observed recovery (fraction of targets with at least one molecule reaching ECFP4-TS $\geq\tau$, averaged over $\tau \in [0.25,0.50]$). The ECFP4 axis is that raw recovery, not a rescaling of it: recovery is already the area under the per-target retention curve and lies in [0,1], so no cross-method normalisation is needed. Panel~B gives the seven normalised scores plotted, and the final panel gives strain coverage: the percentage of each method molecule pool whose strain could be computed within the budget and stayed below $10^{4}$~kcal~mol$^{-1}$. Failures are counted in the denominator of pass rates. NA2smol appears under both Fig.~2d and Fig.~3a because the full model is the same output set in both.
