\section*{Supplementary Table 5 | Chemical overlap of benchmark reference and generated molecules with the training data}
Exact canonical identity and maximum ECFP4 Tanimoto similarity (TS; Morgan radius 2, 2,048
bits, no chirality) were measured against the fine-tuning training partition and the ligand-only
pretraining library. \textbf{A}, reference active ligands. \textbf{B}, NA2smol generated
molecules. In Panel B, all entries are percentages with 95\% Wilson intervals; the pretraining
rows are estimated from a stratified sample of generated molecules (Methods), whereas the
fine-tuning rows use all unique generated molecules. Percentages are over the $n$ given for that
benchmark. The pretraining library carries no target--pocket labels, so the pretraining rows
measure similarity to the ligand chemical space rather than exposure to the evaluated
target--ligand associations; exact identity is a lower bound on chemical familiarity because
different SMILES can encode equivalent structures.
